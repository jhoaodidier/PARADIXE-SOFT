Project overview

Qulla-Ruta

Road resilience and emergency logistics in the Peruvian Andes.

When a landslide cuts the road through Carpish, how can patients and perishable freight keep moving?

The corridor

When the road
closes.

The road through Carpish links the Huánuco highlands with the rainforest around Tingo María.

A single landslide can delay an ambulance and strand a truckload of fresh produce. The two vehicles need different ways through.

System design

From a signal
to a decision.

The proposed system answers three questions before a vehicle moves.

It is designed for intermittent connectivity. Field crews and local authorities verify conditions and make the operational decisions.

Road vulnerability

What makes a road
vulnerable?

The Road Vulnerability Index (IVV) combines four factors into a score from 0 to 100.

Rainfall carries the most weight. The Java prototype uses fixed weights, so the same inputs and model version give the same score.

Carpish simulation

Heavy rain
at Carpish.

With 95 mm of rain in 24 hours and 210 mm in 72 hours, the simulated road segment scores 91.27.

The red band begins at 70. The demo then introduces a landslide. Its notification firewall logs the alert and blocks delivery to real users.

Vehicle response

One closure,
two responses.

A rural detour can take a 4×4 ambulance yet be too narrow for a heavy truck.

The Carpish scenario illustrates both outcomes. A real cargo transfer requires a corridor with a current field inspection.

Simulated impact

Twelve hours
saved.

In the simulation, transferring the load takes 2 hours. Waiting for the road to reopen takes 14.

The estimate includes 15 km of extra driving and produce lost during handling. Both assumptions affect the result.

Field validation

Taking the model
into the field.

The Java prototype can replay the Carpish scenario. Testing it against local observations is the next step.

Field teams would compare risk scores with recorded incidents and inspect the places where patients or cargo could change vehicles.
